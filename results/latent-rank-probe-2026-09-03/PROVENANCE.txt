## What this is

The readiness item-2 follow-up probe behind `unify-shape-reflection-policy` (plan item P3, geometry
audit finding F3). It is a rank diagnostic, not a power study: the design point is held at the Phase-4
pilot's generator settings (`examples/trajectory_power_study/phase4_pilot_100x199.json` — n=300,
4 stages, p_dmp=0.2, effect 1.00, surgery_censoring=clamp, base_seed=400, 199 permutations) and the
only thing varied is the retained PLS latent rank, via the diagnostic `forced_components` override
added by this change.

Every record produced here carries `component_selection: "forced"` in its integration metadata and is
therefore refused by the study report builder (`assert_production_component_selection`). These runs
are not persisted as study shards and can never resume into one: `evaluation_params` are hashed into
the cell parameter signature.

## Design-point note

Seeds are matched across the ladder: rank r and rank r' at replicate i measure the *same* generated
dataset. The `none` mode is carried as a null control so that any decay generic to rank can be told
apart from decay specific to the orientation surgery.

The `population shape` column is measured at the `population_standardized.joint` checkpoint — the
standardized observed space, not the latent space. The two are not on a common scale, so the rank at
which the latent response crosses the population value is indicative only. The load-bearing evidence
is the decay itself and the rejection-rate collapse.

## Outcome

The orientation→shape response decays steeply with retained rank — 0.0694 → 0.0136 (−80.4%), monotone
through rank 9 and flat from 9 to 12 — and its rejection rate collapses alongside it (0.97 at the
CV-selected rank 3 → 0.09 at rank 12), while the `none` control is flat from rank 4 onward. This
supports the rank-limited-projection account of the response. See
`latent_rank_probe.md` for the table and the decay measurement, and `docs/phase5-readiness.md` item 2
for what it unblocks.
